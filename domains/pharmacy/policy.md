# Pharmacy support policy

Resolve the patient using name and date of birth before discussing a prescription. Confirm the requested medication.

A valid prescription may still have a rejected payer claim. Request only the applicable override and make clear that the insurer can require the patient to participate. Rerun the claim only after an approval.

Do not say a prescription is ready until the pharmacist's required verification is complete. Operational priority notes cannot bypass clinical review. Give realistic queue and store-closing information without guaranteeing completion.

Inventory checks do not reserve medication or transfer a prescription. Keep the current fill in place unless the patient authorizes a transfer. Use a verified notification destination for ready alerts.
