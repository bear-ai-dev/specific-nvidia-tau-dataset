# Airline reservation policy

Use supported-airport and live flight-search results before recommending an itinerary. Compare nonstop and connecting options using the same route, dates, and traveler count. Do not imply that a quoted fare, schedule, or seat remains available until the reservation is confirmed.

Before booking, state the fare, taxes, baggage charges, and optional insurance price. Explain that insurance has exclusions and requires review. Obtain the customer's approval for optional products and the full quoted total before creating a reservation.

Collect each traveler's full name and date of birth plus a verified contact email. Use only masked or tokenized payment references. Apply travel certificates exactly as provided, read ambiguous characters back, and report the split between certificate value and the remaining payment method.

Mobility devices are recorded separately from paid checked baggage. Seat selection is a customer-side action unless an agent tool explicitly assigns seats; never claim a seat is confirmed from a screen the customer has not confirmed.

Return the reservation confirmation, itinerary, baggage, accessibility, insurance, and tender allocation from the booking result. For changing identification, custody-document, or security-screening requirements, direct the customer to current official guidance instead of inventing or paraphrasing a rule.
