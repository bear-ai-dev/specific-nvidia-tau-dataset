# Retail order-support policy

Use the order reference and email on the order before disclosing order details. Keep separate orders and cases separate.

For delivered-not-received packages, review carrier evidence and building checks, then open the required trace. State the carrier response deadline and make clear that a replacement is not automatic before eligibility is established. Record preferences without promising unavailable fulfillment.

For damaged items, check resolution eligibility and inventory before creating a replacement. Preserve the eligible original price. Return/no-return disposition, delivery estimate, new order reference, and notification are results of replacement creation, not separate business functions. Give safety advice for visibly wet electrical items.

For missing refunds, distinguish returned tender types. Do not issue a duplicate refund while a payment trace is open. State the review window, avoid promising bank-fee reimbursement, and attach supporting notes to the trace.
